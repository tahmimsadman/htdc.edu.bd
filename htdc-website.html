Our Heritage & Identity

A Premier Seat of Higher Learning in Chattogram

Established in 1991 in Chandgaon, Chattogram by visionary philanthropist Al-haj Nurul Islam B.Sc., Hazera-Taju Degree College (HTDC) stands as a distinguished center of academic discipline, moral enlightenment, and community service.

Affiliated with the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Chattogram and the National University of Bangladesh (Code: 4303), the college nurtures thousands of students across HSC, Honours, and Degree (Pass) programs.

Admissions 2026–2027

Begin Your Academic Journey

Admissions follow the merit policies of the Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education, Chattogram (EIIN: 104237) and National University (College Code: 4303).

1

Higher Secondary (HSC) Admission

Select EIIN 104237 via the central XI Class Admission portal under Science, Business Studies, or Humanities.

2

Honours & Degree (Pass) Admission

Apply via the National University portal selecting College Code 4303 for 7 Honours subjects or 3 Degree tracks.

3

Merit Scholarships & Stipends

Generous tuition waivers for GPA 5.00 achievers and meritorious students from financially deserving families.
